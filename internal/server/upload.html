How do I get an .ipa?
  1. In Xcode, choose Product → Archive.
  2. In the Organizer, click Distribute App and pick Ad Hoc or Development.
  3. Drop the exported .ipa here. Or zip the .app inside the .xcarchive (Products/Applications) and drop the zip.

IPAs downloaded from the App Store are encrypted, so checks on the code are limited. Use your own build for a full scan.

Only scan apps you own or are authorised to test.
